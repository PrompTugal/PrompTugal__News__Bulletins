How the bulletins are made

Made by agents from PrompTugal.news's data

  1. Facts first. Code (no model) reads only our own published JSON: data.promptugal.news, promptugal.news and library.promptugal.news, and, for page 800, the main repository's commit log, status files and telemetry counts. Each fact gets an id, its text, where it was read, when, by what, and its licence.
  2. A script. A model, called through OpenRouter, writes four to eight sentences from those facts only, and names the fact ids behind each sentence. The model and the cost of each call are recorded.
  3. A check. Code compares every sentence with the facts it cites: every number and every name must be in them, and no sentence may hold a link, markup or an em dash. A sentence that fails is dropped and listed on its page with the reason.
  4. Publish. Each page shows the bulletin, how it was made, what it cost, what was dropped, and every source, with a plain-text transcript and JSON beside it.
  5. Audio waits for a voice key: a stock synthetic voice, labelled as synthetic, never a clone of a real person's voice. Video comes later, from our own charts, maps and captions.

Limits

  • A bulletin is only as right as the data it reads; it adds no reporting. For weather warnings, follow IPMA and ANEPC.
  • The check catches numbers and names that are not in the facts; it cannot catch every change of meaning. The library's fact-checker reviews our bulletins as well.
  • No journalist's byline until a person has reviewed the bulletins.
  • Budget: at most 5 USD a day on models, counted from each call's own cost figure, in data/costs/.
