How this video is made

How the lab's videos are made, told by the twelve synthetic presenters: one web page per frame, the 3D half drawn in software by Mesa llvmpipe on a virtual screen, four browsers sharing the frames, and ffmpeg joining pictures and voice. The voice and the presenters are synthetic; no real person.
